The controller only admits a node when its result report contains a bare,
machine-parseable status line matching this exact pattern (multiline regex):

  ^status:\s*(PASSED|NOT_AVAILABLE|BLOCKED)\s*$

That is the harness's own generic "did this node complete" vocabulary,
separate from your own domain verdict vocabulary (`ACTIVATED`,
`IMPLEMENTED_NOT_ACTIVATED`, `BLOCKED`) — the two only happen to share the
spelling "BLOCKED" by coincidence, and your own report currently uses
`## VERDICT: \`IMPLEMENTED_NOT_ACTIVATED\`` as a markdown heading, which the
harness's regex does not match at all (it is not a bare `status:` line, and
`IMPLEMENTED_NOT_ACTIVATED`/`ACTIVATED` are not harness-recognized values
regardless of formatting).

Add a bare, separate `status:` line near the top of your report (after the
title, on its own line, exactly as shown, with nothing else on that line),
in addition to — not instead of — your own domain verdict heading/section,
mapped as follows:

  - Your verdict is `ACTIVATED` or `IMPLEMENTED_NOT_ACTIVATED` -> the line
    reads exactly `status: PASSED`. Both are genuine, complete, valid
    conclusions of a fully-executed audit; the harness has no separate
    concept for them, and `PASSED` here means "this node did its job
    correctly and reached a defensible conclusion," not "the product is
    fully live" — that distinction stays recorded in your own verdict
    section, which a reader must still consult for the real disposition.
  - Your verdict is `BLOCKED` -> the line reads exactly `status: BLOCKED`,
    matching the harness's own non-admission semantics for a node whose own
    audit found a real, unresolved, routable finding.

Do not change anything else in the report — your verdict section, findings,
evidence, and hashes are unaffected. This is purely adding one bare line so
the controller can parse what you already concluded.
