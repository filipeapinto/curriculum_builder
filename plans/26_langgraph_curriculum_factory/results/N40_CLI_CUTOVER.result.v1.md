## The one declared exception to "no node-body imports"

`_resolve_active_manifest` and `_frozen_input_records`
(`runtime.langgraph_factory.nodes.inputs`) are imported and called once each,
before the graph exists, to fix the identity seed
(`persistence.prepare_episode_invocation`'s `active_manifest_path`) and the
authorization/transport `curriculum_digest` a `RuntimeContext` needs before its
first invocation. Both are pure, side-effect-free hashing/selection functions —
not a node body, not a guard, not a routing or acceptance decision. The graph's
own input-freezing node independently recomputes the same values inside the
episode and is the sole authority the graph trusts; a drift between the CLI's
pre-computed digest and the graph's own recomputed one fails closed (an
authorization/curriculum-digest mismatch), it never succeeds silently. Reusing
the functions, rather than re-implementing the hashing rule a second time, is
what keeps the two computations from silently drifting apart.
