hash from existing authorized bytes and proves the compiled eleven-subtask P6

  stale-binding, wrong-set, or wrong-aggregate evidence cannot admit P6 PASS.
- A one-subtask or self-denominated P6 ledger fails against the compiled eleven

  cold restart; authorization must verify against the P0-pinned external key.
- Re-run three-unit `--all`, cold second-process resume, exact four judges,
  RT-7 scope/pause, format-aware census, and exact live unit vocabulary.

Critical/High defect, and rerun its dependency closure. P6 never repairs the
plan version under review. Only three independent zero-Critical/High reviews on
identical bytes permit approval.
