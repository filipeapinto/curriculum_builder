# Write complete unit content

## Job

Write the complete seven-block unit artifact for one selected manifest unit. Derive
all factual content from the accepted domain artifact and admitted source locators.
Do not accept or review your own work.

## Authorized inputs

- activation envelope and validated routing decision;
- the selected manifest-unit projection and its declared visual roles;
- one accepted, hash-bound domain artifact;
- admitted source locators needed for sourced-claim records;
- `schemas/lab.schema.v4.json` as staged by the controller;
- the staged engine calibration and the staged contents of
  `meta_prompt/assets/unit_prose.v1.md` and
  `meta_prompt/assets/pedagogy.v1.md`; and
- the exact output contract and writable target.

Reviewer history, prior rejected prose, sibling unit bodies, hidden fixtures,
controller transitions, and terminal state are excluded.

## Output

Write one complete unit artifact containing all required `identity`, `pedagogy`,
`sequence`, `content`, `safety`, `visuals`, and `domain` blocks. The `domain` block
must reproduce the accepted domain artifact exactly. Include the schema-required
derivation and sourced-claim records so every rendered fact has one resolvable domain
parent and every numeric or safety-critical claim has an admitted source locator.

The content must implement the manifest unit's learner outcome, required explanation,
core activity, safety focus, QA focus, and visual roles. It must follow the calibrated
learner, readability, pedagogy, Predict-Observe-Explain, vocabulary, adult/child role,
and safety contracts supplied to this activation.

## Bounds

- Do not add domain facts outside the accepted domain artifact.
- Do not describe an observation or physical check as performed unless admitted
  evidence records it.
- Do not create, hash, or approve visual files; declare their exact roles, briefs,
  parent facts, placement, alt text, and provenance needs.
- Do not run or report schema, verifier, readability, derivation, render, review, or
  acceptance results.
- Do not choose another node or change counters, routes, evidence, or state.
- Write no file except the declared output.

Complete only when the entire artifact—not an outline or partial chapter—is written.
The controller validates it and determines the next activation.
