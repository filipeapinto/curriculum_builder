1. Mandate and boundary

Research question. How should this repository capture, explain, trace, and learn from failed or wasteful LLM executions without reinventing mature observability, incident-management, durable-execution, and evaluation mechanisms?

The motivating incident reportedly consumed roughly 20% of weekly usage overnight while generating graph-system documentation and revising its generic documentation skill. The investigation will reconstruct that event only from repository evidence and explicitly supplied usage records. The reported percentage is a hypothesis until corroborated.

This plan authorizes research artifacts only. It does not authorize changes to the graph-documentation skill, runtime, schemas, policies, or governance. Those changes require a later implementation decision.

2. Questions the research must resolve

L

Log

Which append-only events, resource measures, decisions, artifacts, and missing-evidence states must be recorded—and at what granularity?

T

Trace

How should runs, agents, model calls, tool calls, handoffs, retries, evaluations, checkpoints, approvals, and artifacts be causally linked?

I

Infer

How can evidence support root-cause hypotheses, counterfactuals, recurrence fingerprints, regression fixtures, and justified changes?

G

Govern

Which budgets, circuit breakers, escalation thresholds, retention rules, gates, and promotion criteria should flow to version N+1?

A

Adopt

Which mature standards or tools should be adopted, adapted, or rejected, and what is the smallest repository-native interoperability layer?

5. Evidence and synthesis method

Evidence hierarchy

  1. Standards and primary specifications
  2. Peer-reviewed or original research
  3. Official platform documentation
  4. Mature open-source implementations
  5. High-quality engineering incident reports
  6. Vendor claims, explicitly labeled and independently triangulated

Claim discipline

  • Each material claim maps to a source.
  • Retrieval date and source class are recorded.
  • Quoted text stays within copyright limits.
  • Facts, interpretations, and recommendations remain distinct.
  • Conflicting evidence receives a disposition, not silent averaging.

Coverage frame

The research will evaluate OpenTelemetry-style observability and GenAI semantic conventions, W3C trace correlation, durable-execution journals and checkpoints, experiment and artifact lineage, evaluation frameworks, SRE incident learning, AI risk governance, and privacy/security controls. Named mechanisms are research targets, not predetermined endorsements.

Failure ontology requirement

The final ontology must distinguish at least: symptom, failed stage, proximate cause, contributing condition, violated control, impact, detectability, evidence quality, recoverability, recurrence fingerprint, and corrective-action class. “Successful but economically unreasonable” must be representable independently of terminal success or failure.

Synthesis gate

A10 may promote a recommendation only when it states the incident relevance, evidence strength, expected preventive or detective effect, adoption cost, portability, privacy implications, falsification test, and proposed governance destination. Unresolved disagreement remains visible.

6. Controls on the research itself

Bounded fan-out

One initial assignment per domain. Follow-up is permitted only for a named evidence gap or reviewer finding.

No blind retries

A failed model call may be repeated only after its failure is logged and the input, hypothesis, tool path, or model decision changes.

Persist immediately

Each agent writes a bounded report before synthesis. Completed work is reused by identifier and digest.

Stop conditions

Stop on sufficient evidence, source saturation, hard budget, repeated failure fingerprint, missing authority, or an unresolved human decision.

Cost visibility

Usage, duration, retries, sources, and artifacts are attributed per agent. Missing provider telemetry is recorded as missing.

Human boundaries

Research approval does not imply implementation approval. Material governance changes return to a human gate.

Pre-run budget record. Before fan-out, the orchestrator must write a machine-readable budget specifying maximum model calls, maximum retry calls, wall-clock deadline, per-agent source ceiling, and escalation threshold. Numeric limits are deliberately not invented in this plan; they require the approver’s available-usage context.
