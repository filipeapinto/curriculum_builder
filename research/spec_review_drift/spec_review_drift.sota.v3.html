Recursive LLM spec drift

Is the issue real? Is it recognized? Are solutions already available? A trigger-agnostic audit of academic evidence, gray literature, counterevidence, and solution maturity.

The precise answer

It is an evidence-supported composite risk—not yet a settled named syndrome. Its mechanisms and neighboring phenomena are well documented. Existing controls address important parts, but no mature product solves retained human intent end to end.

01 · Executive answers

Three questions, stated without ambiguity

1Yes, conditionallyIs it a valid issue?

Yes. Repeated model-mediated transformation can distort information, converge to attractors, amplify self-bias, induce conformity, and select through biased evaluators. But direct longitudinal evidence on real specifications drifting from original human intent remains limited.

2Yes, fragmentedIs it in the literature?

Yes. Academic literature names semantic drift, iterative information distortion, conformity, self-correction failure, preference leakage, policy drift, and specification gaming. Gray literature recognizes context rot, lost decisions, prompt/eval regression, inconsistent artifacts, and the need for versioned controls.

3Components existAre there solutions?

Yes for components: versioned anchors, held-out evals, independent-first review, executable contracts, traces, hooks, permissions, structured specs, and human approvals. No surveyed mature tool independently proves that human intent survived recursive revision.

02 · Validity of the issue

Direct evidence exists—but the exact phenomenon is under-studied

Direct
medium
Iterated transmission distorts content and converges to model-dependent attractors.

“Telephone game” studies directly pass text through model chains and observe amplification or convergence in measurable properties. When LLMs Play the Telephone Game · LLM as a Broken Telephone

Direct
medium
Iterative optimization literature explicitly identifies semantic drift.

RIOT treats semantic drift as a problem in repeated prompt modification and preserves unresolved residual differences. ACL 2025

Direct
medium
Multi-agent interaction produces conformity and persona/opinion instability.

Agents can yield to peer pressure, confabulate, or fail to preserve assigned viewpoints. Persona Inconstancy · ACL 2025 conformity

Direct
medium-high
Self-refinement can increase polish while amplifying self-bias.

Across multiple model families, iterative self-refinement improved fluency and understandability while amplifying preference for the model’s own generations; accurate external feedback improved reliability. ACL 2024

Adjacent
strong
Evaluator and context mechanisms provide a credible causal path.

Evidence covers correlated errors, position bias, self-preference, generator–judge lineage leakage, context rot, and specification gaming. Correlated errors · Preference leakage · Context engineering

Do not overclaim: current evidence does not show that recursive review inevitably destroys intent. Self-Refine and other work show meaningful gains on bounded tasks. Sparse communication and heterogeneous reasoning can also improve some multi-agent systems. The risk rises when the objective is subjective, inherited, or co-evolves with its evaluator.
03 · Literature status

“Recursive LLM specification drift” is not a standardized research category. The literature is real but fragmented across several fields. That distinction matters: it supports the risk without pretending a mature unified discipline already exists.

04 · Existing solutions

Solutions exist by layer, not as one complete product

The market and literature already provide useful controls. Their maturity differs, and each solves a narrower problem than retained human intent.

Most mature defensible stack: versioned external intent anchor + held-out behavioral/expert evals + independent-first review + executable invariants + action-boundary enforcement + explicit human disposition of semantic changes. Heterogeneous judges, residual optimization, and automated auditors are complements, not substitutes.
05 · What remains unsolved

No mature tool closes the loop end to end

No surveyed product simultaneously supplies a protected human-owned intent source, clause-level revision provenance, blind independent review, behavioral drift measurement across versions, deterministic protected invariants, human disposition of material intent changes, cost/authority envelopes, and an evidence-based stopping rule.

Not a solution

More agents or rounds

Homogeneous recursion can amplify shared error and evaluation bias. Agent count is not measured independence.

Not a solution

One large rubric

A rubric may encode the wrong proxy, become a gaming target, or share lineage with the artifact it judges.

Not a solution

One final approval

Human authority is ineffective when the decision surface already exceeds human comprehension.

State-of-the-art conclusion: recursive LLM review is neither inherently corrective nor inherently drifting. It is an adaptive, correlated optimization process. Faithfulness must be demonstrated against preserved external intent anchors and held-out outcomes; consensus and internal consistency are insufficient.
