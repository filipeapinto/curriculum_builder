04 · Application judgment

v11 is evidence, not yet authority

KEEP AS EVIDENCE

v1–v11 lineage

Do not discard the work. It records design knowledge, risks, tests, and operational controls that may remain valuable.
WITHHOLD AUTHORITY

Full execution

The current evidence does not establish that the transformation into a three-skill family was explicitly authorized or still desired.
SEEK INFORMATION

Divergence probe

Compare independent reconstructions from distinct evidence boundaries before spending on implementation.

Inference: the highest-risk possibility is not that v11 is poorly engineered. It is that v11 is a well-engineered solution to a product definition that emerged recursively. Because v11 already has strong downstream safeguards, adding more controls to it would yield little information about this upstream uncertainty.

05 · Recommended probe

Maximize information before commitment

  1. FreezeHash v1–v11, schemas, fixtures, prompts, and recoverable transcripts. Set hard time/token/tool limits.
  2. Blind reconstruction AGive a model the earliest prompts and v1, excluding later versions.
  3. Blind reconstruction BUse a fresh owner interview only, with no v11 terminology or architecture.
  4. Blind reconstruction CGive a different model family v11 only. Ask what product it predicts will exist, its costs, dependencies, and non-goals.
  5. Falsify and adjudicateAnonymize outputs; seek concrete contradictions and repository evidence. Show only material divergences to the owner.
Authorization rule: permit only a bounded vertical slice if the owner recognizes the predicted product, the reconstructions agree on that slice’s observable outcome, and no disputed architecture, dependency, publication authority, or cost driver is hidden inside it.
