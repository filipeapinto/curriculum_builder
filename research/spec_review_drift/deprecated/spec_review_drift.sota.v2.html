01 · Method

Three independent fields, one synthesis

The work was divided among three SMEs: long-horizon agent context and memory; LLM evaluation and multi-agent failure; and AI-native software/specification control. Each lane was instructed to remain trigger-agnostic, prefer 2024–2026 primary research and official engineering evidence, state limitations, and distinguish observed evidence from design proposals.

02 · Findings

The control surface is the whole agent loop

An agent dynamically selects actions in a plan–act–observe–adjust loop. Control is therefore distributed across the model, instruction hierarchy, selected context, memory, tools, permissions, evaluation signals, retained history, and stopping rules—not located in a final document alone. This framing is consistent across current Anthropic and OpenAI engineering guidance, though comparative field evidence remains limited.

01

Long context ≠ durable understanding

Relevant material can remain present but lose behavioral influence as context grows. Anthropic reports position/distance effects and describes context rot; scratchpad evidence extraction can help. Evidence

02

Compaction is governance

A compressor selects which uncertainty, prohibition, provenance, and rationale survives. Efficiency results are relative to chosen benchmarks, not latent human intent. ACON · Context folding

03

Memory is an active policy

Retrieval, consolidation, rewriting, and forgetting shape behavior. A stored fact with no retrieval path has no practical control effect. LoCoMo · Structural memory

04

Recursive critique optimizes a proxy

Self-refinement can improve measured quality, but improvement follows the evaluator. It does not establish preservation of an underspecified originating goal. Self-Refine

05

Authority migrates silently

Interpretations, reviewer suggestions, implementation conveniences, and generated tests can acquire normative force without an explicit decision. This is a synthesis finding grounded in nondeterministic instructions and agent objective-pursuit evidence. GitHub · OpenAI

06

Human approval does not scale automatically

People cannot validate agent-volume artifacts by reading everything. Oversight needs compact decision surfaces showing semantic changes, evidence, dissent, reversibility, and requested authority. Weak-to-strong

03 · Correlated measurement

More reviewers are not more independence

A recursive review system should be treated as a correlated, adaptive measurement system. Models can share training distributions, provider lineage, prompts, summaries, intermediate artifacts, and evaluators. A 2025 study of more than 350 models found substantial conditional error agreement, weakening naïve majority-vote confidence. Correlated Errors in LLMs

Evidence-backed warning: LLM judges show position, familiarity, anchoring, and self-preference biases; related generator–judge lineages create preference leakage. Detailed rubrics improve decomposition but do not remove these effects. Position bias · Self-preference · Preference leakage

04 · Synthesis proposal

A reusable LLM-native control architecture

No cited source validates this integrated architecture as a whole. It is a reasoned synthesis of the mechanisms above. Its central rule is that generated artifacts may propose authority changes but cannot silently enact them.

01 · AUTHORITY

Intent kernel

Human-owned outcomes, non-goals, protected invariants, tradeoffs, decision rights, and unacceptable failures.
02 · KNOWLEDGE

Evidence + interpretation

Immutable sources are distinct from replaceable model hypotheses, summaries, plans, and critiques.
03 · ACTION

Authority envelope

Least-privilege tools, mutation classes, spend/time/delegation caps, approvals, stopping rules, and rollback.
04 · LEARNING

Evaluation portfolio

Deterministic checks, behavioral tests, falsifiers, heterogeneous judges, human decisions, and append-only outcomes.
Append-only provenance: preserve a graph of source → interpretation → decision → plan → action → artifact → test, including branches, rejected alternatives, compaction events, dissent, budget use, and supersession. Generated reasoning is supplementary; observable prompts, context, actions, diffs, tool results, and decisions are stronger anchors. OpenAI reports trajectory monitoring can expose failures missed by final-output review, while also warning that monitorability is imperfect. Source

05 · Reusable invariants

Rules that survive platform changes

A

Proposal is not authority

No model-generated summary, test, or repeated statement becomes authoritative without a designated decision.

B

Compaction is reconstructable

Every compaction is versioned; protected intent, prohibitions, dissent, and provenance cannot be irreversibly evicted.

C

Agreement is calibrated

Review lanes are not called independent without measured error complementarity and lineage awareness.

D

Tests are provenance-aware

An evaluation records who or what generated it, what source it operationalizes, and whether it is held out from optimization.

E

Actions are bounded

Autonomy ends at semantic novelty, irreversible effect, budget breach, protected-intent change, or unresolved disagreement.

F

Convergence means evidence

Smaller diffs, longer prose, checklist completion, and model consensus are not convergence without new external evidence or reduced uncertainty.

What current research does not establish

Central research gap: the field lacks long-duration, ecologically valid studies that measure preservation of human intent across dozens of model-mediated revisions. Vendor sources document real engineering patterns but are not independent validation; several 2025–2026 results remain preprints.
