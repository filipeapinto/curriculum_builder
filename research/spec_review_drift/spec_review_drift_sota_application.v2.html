02 · The three questions applied

What the evidence means here

1

Is the issue valid locally?

Yes as a risk, not as a proven diagnosis. The owner reports loss of the mental model; the artifact underwent repeated model review; its product shape expanded early; and downstream tests evolved with it. Missing iteration rationale prevents ruling drift in or out.

2

Does literature support the concern?

Yes, conditionally. Direct literature shows iterative distortion, self-bias, conformity, and semantic drift. Adjacent work covers context loss and biased evaluators. No study proves this particular specification drifted.

3

Do existing solutions apply?

Yes, in layers. Preserve versions, compare blind reconstructions, use independent external scenarios, retain provenance, enforce bounded execution, and route semantic changes to the owner. No product can infer the owner’s lost intent automatically.

05 · Bounded divergence probe

The next action

01 · FREEZE

Preserve lineage

Hash artifacts and set the research budget. No changes to v11.
02 · RECONSTRUCT

Three blind lanes

Early evidence; fresh owner interview; v11-only interpretation. Prefer distinct model families.
03 · FALSIFY

Find witnesses

Compare predicted outcomes, non-goals, architecture, dependencies, authority, and cost against repository evidence.
04 · DECIDE

Owner disposition

Authorize one reversible slice, reconstruct a candidate, or abandon the family.
Go criterion: authorize only a bounded vertical slice when the owner recognizes the predicted product and no disputed architecture, dependency, permission, or cost driver is hidden within the slice.
06 · Decision

Pause is justified; condemnation is not

The literature does not prove v11 drifted, and the local evidence does not prove it is wrong. It does establish that reciprocal LLM review lacks an inherent fidelity guarantee, that the artifact’s product shape changed materially, and that its tests share its later lineage. Because implementation cost may be substantial and a blind divergence probe is cheap and reversible, the rational decision is to pause full execution and buy information first.
