Each finding has a stable ID. “Strength” describes the evidence; “applicability” describes its transfer to this repository. Source-level details and limits are in the machine-readable findings.

F-CSMQG-001

Same-model review is bounded evidence

Fresh contexts and samples may expose defects or disagreement, but empirical evidence also shows instability, self-preference, position/reference sensitivity, and adversarial flips. They are procedural separation, not independent assurance.

Strength: moderate empirical direction. Applicability: high as a risk boundary; local effect unknown.

EVM-001; claims C-003, C-013, C-014, C-018, C-022.

F-CSMQG-002

Executable criteria bind observable behavior

Deterministic validators, properties, hidden tests, metamorphic checks, and mutation probes bind a verdict more directly than ungrounded semantic judgment. Their boundary is the encoded oracle and fault coverage.

Strength: high official + moderate empirical. Applicability: high for code and document structure; under-evidenced for material document/plan semantics.

EVM-003; claims C-005–C-008, C-024, C-025.

F-CSMQG-003

Panels need calibration

Aggregation can reduce some variance and surface disagreement, but needs labels, nonredundant signals, a frozen rule, and explicit abstention. Consensus is not truth; repeats are not new gold cases.

Strength: moderate, conditional and mixed. Applicability: medium; local cost and effect unknown.

Claims C-002, C-003, C-013, C-019, C-020, C-023.

F-CSMQG-004

Integrity is not correctness

Hashes, lineage, rollout comparison, and attestations can bind bytes or signer/subject claims. They do not establish semantic truth, reviewer accuracy, or independent origin.

Local control and repository-fit analysis

The local qa-gate-codex-run boundary was unchanged at revalidation. Its patterns are useful requirements, but its documented success stories remain attributed claims unless supported by raw records.

Requirements derived from inspected source

  • Versioned artifact lineage and pass-time digest binding.
  • Criterion-linked severity threshold and terminal states.
  • Hash-chain and rollout comparison for specified byte/record consistency.
  • Execution-copy preparation, bounded iteration, and a fresh postmortem context.
  • Only two narrow deterministic regressions passed; no carried-forward pattern is a live deployable assurance until proven.

Gaps that block assurance claims

  • Parsed verdicts are not enforced against verdict.schema.json.
  • Current grounding is not rehashed during verification.
  • Postmortem witness material is not authenticated by verification.
  • No live transport, session-resume, sandbox, plugin-version, or reviewer-accuracy evidence.
  • Five declared eval cases have no execution/scoring ledger.

Boundary language: a clean local verification can be described as checked records agreeing with the available current-user rollout. It must not be described as cryptographic provenance, independent origin, or proof that a reviewer PASS is correct.

Same-model QA is insufficient when any condition holds

  1. A material criterion lacks a trusted deterministic, executable, inspectable, or human-adjudicated oracle.
  2. The decision triggers high-impact or irreversible legal, safety, security, privacy, financial, credentialed, or production action.
  3. The same model would be the sole judge of a disputed semantic property of its own or same-family output.
  4. Samples disagree, fail to converge under the frozen rule, or abstain.
  5. Prompt injection, unsafe instructions, broader capability, or sandbox conflict is material or unresolved.
  6. Artifact, criteria, grounding, raw output, scorer, session, or provenance cannot be bound and verified.
  7. Required call/token/cost/model/session telemetry is unavailable or a hard stop is reached.
  8. A mandatory deterministic check fails, or a material deviation/model substitution lacks prior authority.

Required route: fail, invalid, blocked, or abstain/escalate as specified—never automatic pass. Material document/plan semantics without executable or inspectable oracles require human or external adjudication. Security and higher-risk tiers remain blocked until live sandbox/injection proof exists.

Threats, limitations, and residual risks

Evidence limitations

  • Bounded 25-candidate corpus; no full saturation claim.
  • Six official pages excluded for insufficient hash-bound access.
  • Only one low-quality self-refinement source; most empirical sources moderate and not Codex-specific.
  • External executable-assurance evidence is code-heavy; document semantic transfer is weak.
  • Training contamination and model-internal correlation remain unknown.

Operational residuals

  • No local effectiveness, latency, resource, escalation-load, or attack-surface measurement.
  • Same-model systematic error can survive contexts and panels.
  • Deterministic oracles can be incomplete or wrong; generated tests can overfit.
  • Same-user evidence stores may not provide independent provenance.
  • Human authority is scarce and fallible; timeout cannot approve.

Acceptance blockers: zero-run evaluation; unvalidated corpus labels/oracles; unproven telemetry feasibility; frozen V3 wording awaiting a superseding record; proposed tests not executable specifications; absent human/shadow baselines; unproven sandbox/security enforcement; deterministic verification not yet passed; no human final decision; implementation explicitly unauthorized.
