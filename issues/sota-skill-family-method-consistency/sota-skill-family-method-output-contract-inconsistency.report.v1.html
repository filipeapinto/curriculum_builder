Confirmation conclusion

Verdict: CONFIRMED · Scope change: EXPANDED · Evidence sufficiency: SUFFICIENT

The central issue is confirmed: an execution attributed to this family does not inherit one enforceable package, schema, lifecycle, verification, and research-answer contract. The investigation expanded the issue with direct contemporary defects in both runs and narrowed one overstatement: the family already has meaningful generic invariants, but they are too underspecified and plan-dependent to provide comparable conformance.

Impact and severity rationale

Investigated severity: SEV-2_CRITICAL; priority: P0. The defect can produce false assurance, incompatible terminal states, unreliable resume/acceptance discovery, and non-comparable research packages across every SOTA-family execution. It is not classified SEV-1 because no immediate safety catastrophe or production outage is evidenced. Severity reflects consequence; P0 reflects remediation priority.

Applicable expectation and decision criterion

A later plan should establish one family-owned canonical record root, schema/ID/provenance contract, append-only lifecycle, digest-bound challenge and authority records, non-waivable verifier baseline, per-question research-answer vocabulary, separate execution/verification/acceptance/implementation states, typed domain extensions, and plan strengthen-only rules. This is a planning input, not an implementation design or authorization.
