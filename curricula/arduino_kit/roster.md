# No-Arduino Electronics Workbook: Lab Roster

Scope assumption: current ELEGOO UNO R3 Super Starter Kit inventory. The UNO R3 controller is deliberately excluded. Labs use the breadboard power module with a regulated 5 V USB source and a basic digital multimeter. A USB logic analyzer or a simple PWM tester is marked optional where a module cannot be fully verified by hand.

## Foundations and connections

1. Safe low-voltage power: USB supply, 9 V clip, and breadboard power module
2. The breadboard: hidden connections and a continuity investigation
3. Jumper wires and the breadboard expansion board: routes, pin labels, and fault finding
4. Multimeter essentials: voltage, resistance, continuity, and current limits

## Passive parts, light, and manual inputs

5. Resistors: colour code, tolerance, and current limiting
6. Rectifier diodes (1N4007): one-way current and polarity
7. Single-colour LEDs: polarity, brightness, and forward voltage comparison
8. RGB LED: three controllable light paths and common pin identification
9. Potentiometer: a hand-controlled voltage divider
10. Photoresistor: converting light changes into voltage changes
11. Thermistor: converting temperature changes into voltage changes
12. Pushbuttons: normally open switches and contact bounce
13. Tilt switch: gravity as an electrical input
14. Joystick module: two potentiometers and a pushbutton in one device

## Electronic switching and sound

15. PN2222 transistor: using a small current to control a larger current
16. Five-volt relay: an electrically controlled isolated switch
17. Active buzzer: making sound from steady DC
18. Passive buzzer: why a changing signal makes a tone

## Display and manual digital logic

19. One-digit seven-segment display: LEDs arranged to make numbers
20. Four-digit seven-segment display: shared segments and multiplexing
21. 74HC595 shift register: manually storing and moving eight binary states
22. LCD1602: power, contrast, backlight, and the need for a timed command language

## Sensors and remote signals

23. Infrared remote: identifying invisible light safely with a phone camera
24. IR receiver: detecting encoded infrared bursts
25. Ultrasonic sensor: time of flight and why fast timing matters
26. DHT11: a digital temperature/humidity sensor and its data conversation

## Motion and output modules

27. Servo motor: position commands made from pulse width
28. Stepper motor: coils, magnetic steps, and the four-step sequence
29. ULN2003 driver board: safely switching a motor coil from low-power controls

## Consolidation and diagnostic practice

30. LED + switch circuit: predict, build, measure, and explain a complete loop
31. Light-controlled transistor switch: a sensor controlling an output
32. Temperature threshold investigation: calibrating a thermistor divider
33. Relay driver design: transistor, diode protection, and low-voltage load only
34. Signal detective: classify analogue, switch, pulse, and serial signals
35. Breadboard repair shop: diagnose five intentionally faulty circuits

Every lab contains at least three labelled infographics: identify, build, and observe. Labs 20, 22, and 24-27 include a fourth timing/protocol infographic.
