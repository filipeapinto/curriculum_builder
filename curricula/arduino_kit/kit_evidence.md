# ELEGOO UNO R3 Super Starter Kit — reference evidence v1

## Identified kit

The curriculum inventory matches the **ELEGOO UNO R3 Super Starter Kit**. The official product listing includes the same core items used by the 35-lab curriculum: the UNO R3 board, breadboard, power-supply module, 9 V battery with DC connector, DHT11, ultrasonic sensor, servo, stepper motor and ULN2003 board, relay, IR remote and receiver, LCD1602, joystick, seven-segment displays, 74HC595, LEDs, resistors, diode, transistor, buzzer, photoresistor, thermistor, and tilt switch.

## Source files

- `official_kit_photo.jpg` — official product photograph downloaded from ELEGOO’s product CDN. It shows the actual kit family, including the power module at upper left, the 9 V battery and DC lead at right-centre, and the breadboard across the middle.

## Verified reference facts

- The supplied source is a **9 V battery with DC connector**, not a 9 V/1 A wall adapter.
- The kit contains a breadboard power-supply module and warns not to use a voltage higher than 9 V.
- The official product photo shows the module’s independent selector positions labelled `3.3`, `OFF`, and `5`, and its power switch. These are visual reference facts, not generated labels.
